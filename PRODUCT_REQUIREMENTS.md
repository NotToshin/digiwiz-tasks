# Product requirements

## Who and why

Digiwiz Tasks is a staff-only workspace for roughly five people across DIGIWIZ's teams. It replaces a manual job tracker with company records, project and task progress, and a reliable view of daily and monthly working hours. Employees can use any email provider. Clients are records inside the app. They have no accounts in the first release.

## Work organization

- **Company profile** holds company name, logo, optional website and business contact, notes, and counts of projects and tasks by status. Include a distinct internal DIGIWIZ company record for agency work.
- **Project** belongs to a company. It includes a lead, team members, status, optional start and target dates, project summary, and progress. Authorized staff can create projects and assign teams. The company page shows active and completed projects and all jobs the viewer can access.
- **Task** belongs to one project and inherits its company. Include title, date assigned, work type, details, status, delivery date, notes, priority, assignees, optional reviewer, start date where helpful, and audit history. Support checklists, subtasks, dependencies, comments and mentions, private attachments and file links, recurring work, and task-time tracking in release one.
- An editable work-type list starts with website design, website development, UI/UX, WordPress development, software development, graphic design, branding, video editing, motion graphics, audio editing, content writing, copywriting, social media, paid ads, SEO, analytics and reporting, QA, maintenance, and other. Keep work types distinct from departments and permissions.
- **Activity** shows what people are working on and what changed. Every authorized project and company page links to its filtered activity. Events cover task creation, assignment and deadline changes, status, review decisions, and progress updates. Current work uses task status and optionally an active task timer.
- **Example** for a website project: Rian owns UI design, Toshin owns development, and both can be assigned to the handoff task. Set design as a dependency of development and view progress, delivery dates and handoff notes from the client company profile. Names in sample data illustrate the workflow.

## Dashboard and communication

The home screen prioritizes the running work-hours counter, tasks due today, and project progress. Calendar is the first full workspace view, followed by Board and Timeline. Include My Tasks, Activity, Notifications, Companies, and role-appropriate Settings. Deliver in-app assignment, mention, deadline, review-request, and approval notifications. Returning a task for changes also notifies its assignees. Use email for account registration OTP and password recovery.

## Pilot and release target

**Three must-haves** are company-to-task visibility, calendar-based attendance with correct monthly totals, and multi-person assignment through lead approval. Pilot a live website project using design and development tasks with two teammates and one lead or administrator. Run for one working week, capture at least one full handoff and approval, and compare attendance with the team's actual check-in and check-out times, including a missed logout drill. Aim to have the pilot after Phase 5. Set a calendar launch date once the team completes the pilot and Hostinger access is verified.
